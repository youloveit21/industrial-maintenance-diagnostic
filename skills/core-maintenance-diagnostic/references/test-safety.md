# Test safety

Diagnostic value never overrides electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, mechanical, stored-energy, arc-flash, or unexpected-motion controls.

- Follow the site's energy-control program, qualified-person rules, PPE requirements, and the equipment manufacturer's procedures.
- Treat 50 V and above, exposed energized conductors, 480 V systems, DC buses, heater circuits, capacitors, and servo drives as hazardous. Use correctly rated leads, probes, meter, PPE, and approach boundaries.
- De-energize, lock out/tag out, release or block stored energy, and verify absence of voltage before resistance, continuity, connector removal, or mechanical access unless an authorized documented procedure requires otherwise.
- Prove the meter on a known source before and after an absence-of-voltage test when applicable.
- Do not instruct bypassing, jumpering, forcing, defeating, or modifying safety devices for production operation. Safety diagnostics follow OEM/site procedures and require controlled validation.
- Block or secure gravity loads and account for accumulators, trapped pressure, springs, hot surfaces, rotating parts, and automatic restart.
- Do not recommend live current measurements by opening a conductor when a clamp method or controller diagnostic can answer the question safely.
- If exact test points cannot be identified from labels or prints, request a clear photo or schematic rather than guessing.

When the user's training, authorization, meter rating, or site conditions are unknown, clearly identify the hazard and provide the observation/document check that can be done safely while reserving hazardous work for a qualified person.
